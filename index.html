All In One Electric: Website Redesign

Problem:a family-run electrical contractor’s website had an unreadable logo, six different names for its main action, two conflicting quote forms, and trust signals buried at the bottom.

Role:ui/ux designer (client project, 2025)

Impact

  • 4 pages redesigned: home, about, services and ev charging
  • 6 → 2 calls to action: get a quote and schedule an appointment
  • 2 → 1 quote forms merged into one consistent form
05

EDEN: A Smart Dual-Zone Terrarium

Problem:houseplant owners struggle to keep plants alive because sensors only notify and assume one ideal climate. EDEN autonomously manages dual zones for conflicting plant needs while giving users glanceable, legible feedback.

Role:dashboard UI design, evaluation lead, documentation and demo production

Impact

  • 4.1/5 trust rating for sensing and status feedback
  • 6/6 correct soil-classification results in evaluation
  • low workload on alert resolution, which shaped dashboard alerts and action design
